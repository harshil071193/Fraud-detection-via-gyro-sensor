    A genuine inspection walks once around one car: the photo positions go round a car-sized ring, the camera keeps turning the same way, and the camera points at the middle of the ring.     Fraud signals and score

    Each signal scores 0–1 and is multiplied by its weight; the total is out of 100. Scores of 55+ are flagged as likely fraud, 30–54 need review.

    These are heuristics. The thresholds were set from only two recordings on one device (Galaxy A26, Android), so treat the score as a guide and tune it with more labelled inspections before using it to block anyone.
